# Changelog

This project uses Semantic Versioning: MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH, with a suffix such as `-beta.1` for test releases.

## [0.2.0-beta.1] - 2026-11-06

### Added
- One-tap story maker: pick a story, friend, lesson, language, age and length, and the app makes the whole book with pictures, offline.
- Surprise me button for a random book.
- Eight stories in English, Filipino and Taglish.

### Changed
- New bright home screen and two-column bookshelf for phones.
- Books are saved and opened right after they are made.

## [0.1.0-beta.1] - 2026-11-06

First test release. Not yet tested on a real phone.

### Added
- Portrait-only, offline Android app. Requires Android 8.0 or newer.
- Make picture books with your own words, pictures and hand drawings.
- Bookshelf saved on the phone, with search and a sample book.
- Tiko the tarsier as the main character.
- Reader with five read-aloud voices and word highlighting.
- Save a book as a PDF.
- Automatic updates from GitHub Releases. The app downloads a newer version and installs it after you leave the app.

### Known limits
- Claude story writing and drawing are not in this offline app.
- Android asks for permission to install updates the first time, and may ask you to confirm an update. On Android 12 and newer, later updates can install without asking.
